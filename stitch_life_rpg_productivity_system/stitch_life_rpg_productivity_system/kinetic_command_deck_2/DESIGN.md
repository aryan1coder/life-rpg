## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance productivity experience framed through the high-stakes lens of a cinematic sci-fi command deck and RPG progression interface. It targets disciplined operators, builders, and knowledge-workers who treat real-world mastery as an evolving campaign. The emotional target is focused empowerment, deliberate momentum, and mechanical precision: task completion should not feel like administrative churn, but like acquiring hard-won telemetry, leveling core attributes, and securing high-tier upgrades.

The visual direction merges **refined tactical sci-fi HUD principles with high-end glassmorphism and modern UI utility**. It avoids cartoonish gamification tropes in favor of restrained, premium hardware aesthetics: semi-translucent obsidian cockpit panels, calibrated edge lights, ultra-crisp monospaced telemetry, and deliberate photon glows reserved strictly for progression milestones and rare status triggers. Every interface element exists as a structured module in an atmospheric space where real-world productivity commands the gravitational weight of an epic campaign.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to mimic high-performance tactical heads-up instrumentation against a deep void.

- **Foundational Canvas**: The primary backdrop sits at pure obsidian (`#090A0F`), layered upward into tactical base panels (`#0E1118`) and elevated modules (`#131722` to `#1A1F2C`). Surface borders utilize desaturated gunmetal strokes (`#2A324B` to `#3B4668`) with subtle opacity shifts to delineate spatial planes.
- **Primary Energy (XP, Action & Trajectory)**: Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) escalating to Bioluminescent Violet (`#818CF8` and `#A855F7`). Used for the core operational drivers—primary interactions, progression telemetry, level vectors, and active campaign state markers.
- **Secondary Prestige (Gold, Rarity & Rewards)**: Radiant Sunburst Gold (`#F59E0B`, accented by `#FBBF24` and `#FDE047`). Reserved exclusively for tangible yield: earned currency, rare inventory cache unlocks, elite rank achievements, and active mastery tokens.
- **Tertiary Resonance (Mana, Intellect & Discovery)**: Cryo Cyan (`#06B6D4`). Applied to mental energy reserves, attribute readouts (e.g., INT / DIS metrics), deep work time streams, and passive analytical buffs.
- **Diagnostic Status Lights**:
  - **Success / Completed Objective**: Hyper-Emerald (`#10B981`) for completed encounters and secured objectives.
  - **Streak / Thermal State**: Solar Flare Amber (`#F97316`) for chain momentum, daily survival streaks, and critical timers.
  - **Hazard / Raid Encounter**: Tactical Crimson (`#EF4444`) for high-urgency deadlines, overdue quests, and boss encounter health gauges.
- **Contrast Ratios**: Body text strictly adheres to `#E2E8F0` (Slate-100) and `#94A3B8` (Slate-400), ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against dark slate surfaces while colored photon glows retain luminous clarity.

## Typography

The typographic architecture balances aggressive tactical engineering with frictionless readability:

- **Display & Level Identifiers (`Space Grotesk`)**: Imbues module headers, rank transitions, and quest milestones with an angular, forward-leaning momentum. Headlines employ tight tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to create dense, commanding typographic locks.
- **Telemetry, System Stats & Attributes (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Every numerical metric—XP counts, Gold bounties, cooldown timers, attribute values (STR, INT, DIS, CRE, VIT), and inventory counts—uses fixed-width alignment. This prevents visual jitter during numerical ticks and reinforces the instrumentation feel. Micro-labels default to uppercase styling with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`).
- **Body & Objective Briefings (`Inter`)**: All long-form text, mission descriptions, lore fragments, and input controls rely on Inter for optical clarity against high-contrast glass panels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a **tactical dashboard grid**—a responsive multi-column structural grid system engineered for simultaneous visibility of character metrics, active queues, and battleground status.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column fluid grid. Layout defaults to a 3-column asymmetric cockpit: Left Column (3 cols) for Character Core & Attributes; Center Stage (6 cols) for Active Quests, Encounter Tracks, and Campaign Timelines; Right Column (3 cols) for Tactical Ledger (Daily Streaks, Inventory Loot Cache, Mana Reservoir). Gutter: `1.5rem` (`24px`), Margin: `2rem` (`32px`).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout. Character stats collapse into a high-density top command bar. Center operations expand to 5 columns; ledger modules fit into 3 columns. Gutter: `1rem` (`16px`), Margin: `1.5rem` (`24px`).
  - **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column single-stack stream. Modules dock vertically with swipeable quick-telemetry ribbons. Level and streak metrics anchor into a persistent top HUD strip. Gutter: `0.75rem` (`12px`), Margin: `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Rhythm & Padding**: Strict 4px/8px incremental rhythm. Internal card structures leverage `space-md` (`16px`) and `space-lg` (`24px`) padding, while nested telemetry nodes utilize `space-xs` (`4px`) and `space-sm` (`8px`) for compact spatial density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque stacking or drop shadows; it relies on **spatial luminescence, refractive glass borders, and layered dark matter**.

1. **Layer 0 (Atmosphere / Bedrock)**: Deep solid `#090A0F` canvas overlaid with an ultra-subtle radial gradient tinted with Electric Indigo (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.04)`) originating from the active screen sector.
2. **Layer 1 (Command Deck Panels)**: Translucent charcoal (`rgba(19, 23, 34, 0.72)`) with a `16px` backdrop-blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Bound by a 1px composite outline: `border: 1px solid rgba(42, 50, 75, 0.65)` with an inner top highlight edge (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
3. **Layer 2 (Interactive Quests & Floating Modules)**: Surface raised to `rgba(26, 31, 44, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop blur. Surface is illuminated by ambient glow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. Interactive hover state applies an edge-glow gradient using active mission colors (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`).
4. **Layer 3 (Modals, Stat Inspector & Loot Drops)**: High-translucency obsidian with intense blur (`32px`). Border upgraded to high-contrast cyan/indigo glow ring: `0 0 0 1px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.5), 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **soft-technical profile (`roundedness: 1`)**. Surfaces display slight, intentional corner easing (`4px` to `8px`) rather than rounded or pill geometries, evoking physical avionics hardware, cut crystal optics, and precision tactical computers.

- **Base Components & Inputs**: `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius. Produces a sharp, military-grade instrument feel without harsh industrial brutality.
- **Cards, HUD Containers & Panels**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) corner radius (`rounded-lg`). Creates smooth containment for glass layers while preserving precise corner highlights.
- **Floating Badges, Level Pills & Loot Counters**: Micro-radii of `0.25rem` (`4px`) or clipped corner chamfers (via CSS polygon masks) for high-tier item tags, ensuring rewards look like tangible digital ingots and tokens rather than generic bubble labels.

## Components

### Buttons & Tactical Triggers
- **Primary Mission Button**: Background is a continuous gradient from Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) to Deep Violet (`#4F46E5`). Border is capped with a `1px` top-highlight border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`). Box shadow delivers an energized aura: `0 0 20px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.4)`. Text rendered in `Space Grotesk` (600), uppercase, tracked.
- **Claim Bounty Action**: Gold-tinted action surface (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`) with an amber ambient field (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`). Houses reward pill badges displaying earned yields (`+120 XP`, `+35 GOLD`).
- **Ghost / HUD Wireframe Action**: Transparent backdrop, `1px` tactical border (`rgba(59, 70, 104, 0.8)`), text `#94A3B8`. Hover drives border to `#818CF8` and injects `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.08)` surface wash.

### Quest & Objective Cards
- **Structure**: Glass card (`Layer 1`) featuring a dual-accent horizontal edge bar indicating quest difficulty (Cyan for Rank C, Indigo for Rank A, Gold for Legendary Campaign, Crimson for Boss Encounter).
- **Header**: Quest title in `Space Grotesk` (`title-md`), accompanied by sub-tier category breadcrumbs in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-sm`).
- **Footer**: Integrated telemetry strip showcasing progress rings, countdown ticks, attribute tags (`[+2 DIS]`, `[+1 INT]`), and claim bounties.

### Attribute & Progression Gauges
- **XP Progression Bar**: Dark channel base (`#0E1118`) with a high-glow violet/indigo fill. The leading edge of the progress fill features a pure white flare node (`#FFFFFF`) radiating a 12px circular bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px #818CF8`).
- **Radial Stat Wheels**: Circular SVG indicators for daily completion and Mana pools. Outer track uses `#1E2538`, while active arc is rendered in high-voltage Cyan or Emerald with `stroke-linecap: round` and dropped drop-shadow glow filters.

### Character Identity & Status Node
- **Class Identifier**: Encased in an elevated card with class designations ("Acolyte of Focus", "Grand Artificer") set in `Space Grotesk` with gold/cyan metallic gradients.
- **Attributes Matrix (STR, INT, DIS, CRE, VIT)**: Displayed as compact, fixed-width telemetry rows. Attribute labels in `JetBrains Mono` with individual micro-bars and incremental integer readouts (`stat-numeric`).
- **Streak Node**: Flanked by an energetic Amber flame badge (`#F97316`) housing real-time day counts, with pulse animation applied upon active milestone maintenance.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Command Input**: Solid `#0E1118` field enclosed in `1px solid #2A324B`. Placeholder text in `#64748B`. Focused state engages an internal neon indigo ring (`#6366F1`) and a soft external aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)`).
- **Checkboxes (Objective Nodes)**: Diamond or beveled square markers. Unchecked: `1px solid #3B4668` with charcoal core. Checked: Instant fill with Hyper-Emerald (`#10B981`) or Indigo (`#6366F1`), featuring an inner luminous checkmark and momentary expansion shockwave.

### Reward Pills & Loot Tokens
- Micro-containers with ultra-compact padding (`2px 8px`), dark crystal backgrounds (`rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.8)`), and high-contrast glowing typography (`JetBrains Mono`). Gold reward tokens integrate an amber particle glyph; XP tokens feature an electric spark glyph.
