# LIFE RPG — Stitch Design System Specification

## 1. Design Principles
- **Dark-Only Surface Architecture:** `#0B0E15` (canvas), `#11141C` (shell), `#151923` (card), `#1B1F2A` (elevated).
- **Subtle Boundaries:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Purposeful Color Semantics:**
  - Electric Indigo `#6366F1` — Progression & Primary Execution.
  - Radiant Gold `#F59E0B` — Vault Economy & High Yield Milestones.
  - Emerald `#10B981` — Verified Completion & Streak Cadence.
  - Amber `#F97316` — Streak Reminders & Time Sensitivity.
  - Crimson `#EF4444` — Boss Raid Threat Directives.
- **Typography Scale:**
  - Display: Geist Bold 32px
  - Section Titles: Geist Semibold 24px
  - Card Titles: Geist Semibold 18px
  - Body: Inter Regular/Medium 14px
  - Telemetry / Numeric: JetBrains Mono Semibold 12–20px
